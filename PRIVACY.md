# Privacy Policy for Exclaim!

**Last updated:** January 9, 2026

## Overview
Exclaim! is a browser extension that replaces sentence-ending periods with exclamation points. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection
**Exclaim! does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

## Local Storage
The extension stores your preferences locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API:
- Enabled/disabled state
- Exclamation count setting
- Selected categories
- Custom site allowlist

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

## Permissions Used
- **Storage:** Saves your preferences locally on your device
- **Host access:** Required to modify webpage content (replacing periods with exclamation points)

## Third Parties
This extension does not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics, tracking, or external services.

## Changes to This Policy
Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page.
